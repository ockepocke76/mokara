# 🌺 About Mokara

## A Flower That Shouldn't Exist

The **Mokara orchid** is not found in nature. It's a human-engineered marvel — a
hybrid carefully crafted by crossing three distinct orchid species: *Arachnis*,
*Ascocentrum*, and *Vanda*.

What makes it special? The Mokara is **more vibrant**, **more resilient**, and
**longer-lasting** than any of its natural parents. It was designed with
intention, not left to chance.

## Financial Strategies, Engineered

Like the Mokara orchid, the strategies on this platform are **designed, not
found**.

We don't rely on market luck or traditional one-size-fits-all approaches.
Instead, we **cross-pollinate proven investment principles** — combining
accumulation and withdrawal strategies, blending conservative and aggressive
tactics — then stress-test them across thousands of scenarios.

## Our Mission

We're building the world's most accessible **financial strategy design
platform**, powered by:

- 🤖 **AI-Driven Design** — describe your goals in plain English, get a custom strategy
- 🏆 **Collective Intelligence** — learn from top-performing community strategies
- 🔬 **Rigorous Testing** — stress-test against thousands of Monte Carlo scenarios

**Our Vision:** democratize sophisticated financial planning tools that were
once reserved for institutional investors and ultra-high-net-worth individuals.

## How It Works

1. **Browse** — explore the [leaderboard](/leaderboard) of evaluated strategies.
2. **Simulate** — [run a Monte Carlo simulation](/simulate) with your own
   parameters: initial investment, contributions, withdrawals, time horizon.
3. **Design** — use the AI [strategy designer](/strategies) to create, test,
   and evolve your own strategy, then publish it for evaluation.

## 🏛️ The Three Pillars

### 🔬 The Science

**Monte Carlo Simulations** × **Historical Backtests** × **AI-Powered Design**

Every strategy is stress-tested across **8 standardized market scenarios**,
from extreme bull markets to devastating crashes. We simulate thousands of
years of potential outcomes to find hidden risks before they find you.
→ [Read the Methodology](/docs/methodology)

### 🏆 The Community

**Collective Intelligence** beats individual expertise when the right
conditions exist: clear scoring mechanisms, independent contributors, and
effective aggregation. Our leaderboard harnesses the **Wisdom of the Crowd**,
surfacing strategies that no single financial advisor would design alone.
→ [See Top Strategies](/leaderboard)

### 🌍 Accessible to All

**Democratizing Sophisticated Finance.** Tools once reserved for
institutional investors and ultra-high-net-worth individuals are now
accessible to everyone. No PhD in finance required, no $10M minimum.
Design in plain English, test in minutes, deploy with confidence.
→ [Start Free](/simulate)

## 👥 Who We Are

### 🇸🇪 Built in Stockholm

Born in Sweden's innovation capital, where long winter nights teach
**patience** and the midnight sun reminds us to plan for **abundance**.

Stockholm is home to some of the world's most successful fintech
innovations — from mobile payments to challenger banks. We're continuing
that tradition by bringing institutional-grade financial planning tools to
everyone.

### 🌲 Mokara Values

We embrace ***lagom*** — the philosophy of "just right." Not too much, not
too little.

- **Balance**: blend aggressive growth with conservative protection
- **Sustainability**: long-term thinking over short-term gains
- **Equality**: everyone deserves access to sophisticated tools
- **Transparency**: open methodology, clear scoring, no black boxes

These values guide every decision we make.
